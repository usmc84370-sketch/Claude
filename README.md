# Meridian Empires

A lightweight, cartoon-styled turn-based 4X strategy game for the browser. Open `index.html` — no build step, no server, one file. Web fonts are the only thing that needs a connection.

## What's in it

- Procedural hex maps in three sizes and four types (continents, pangaea, islands, inland sea), fog of war, six nations with their own bonuses, 2–5 AI rivals and roaming raiders, three difficulty levels.
- Start in any of seven ages, or drop into a prebuilt modern world where every nation already has cities, roads and armies.
- Cities with growth, borders, 30 buildings, 10 wonders, projects, a production list and gold purchasing.
- 60 technologies across seven eras and 8 policies bought with culture.
- 37 unit types on land and sea with drawn, animated sprites and role bonuses; promotions, drilling, auto-explore, escorts, upgrades.
- Formations: flanking, suppression, four-unit armies and routing.
- Worker improvements, roads, forest clearing, walls that enemies must breach, and missile silos.
- Nuclear weapons with a full triad (silos, bomber wings, nuclear submarines), massed launches, fallout and AI deterrence.
- Happiness and golden ages, religion with beliefs and missionaries, trade routes, and diplomacy with open borders, luxury and technology deals.
- Win by holding every original capital, launching a starship, or leading on score at turn 400.
- Autosaves every turn, plus three manual save slots. Works with mouse, keyboard and touch, in portrait and landscape.
- A temporary Dev tools panel lives in the Menu.
