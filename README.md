# Meridian Empires

A lightweight, cartoon-styled turn-based 4X strategy game for the browser. Open `index.html` — no build step, no server, one file. Web fonts are the only thing that needs a connection.

## What's in it

- Procedural hex maps in three sizes, fog of war, six nations with their own bonuses, 2–5 AI rivals and roaming raiders, three difficulty levels.
- Cities with growth, borders, 22 buildings, 10 wonders, a production list and gold purchasing.
- 38 technologies across six eras and 8 policies bought with culture.
- 27 unit types on land and sea with drawn, animated sprites; promotions, drilling, auto-explore, upgrades.
- Worker improvements, roads, and walls that enemies must breach.
- Happiness and golden ages, religion with beliefs and missionaries, trade routes, and diplomacy with open borders, luxury and technology deals.
- Win by holding every original capital, launching a starship, or leading on score at turn 300.
- Autosaves to the browser every turn. Works with mouse, keyboard and touch, in portrait and landscape.
