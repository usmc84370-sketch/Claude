Rules & payouts

Ante up (Pair Plus is optional), get three cards, then Play (bet equal to your ante) or Fold (lose ante and Pair Plus). Dealer needs Queen-high or better to qualify. If the dealer doesn't qualify, ante pays 1:1 and the play bet pushes. If the dealer qualifies, the better hand wins ante and play at 1:1; ties push.

Hand ranks: straight flush > three of a kind > straight > flush > pair > high card.
